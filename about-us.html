Cool Square Studio is a creative production company specializing in Digital Humans, 3D Animation, Character & Creature Creation, CGI, VFX, AI Production, Immersive Experiences, Educational Content, Video Restoration, and Custom 3D Printing.

We combine artistic creativity with advanced production technologies to create cinematic visuals, interactive experiences, and production-ready digital content for advertising, entertainment, education, gaming, and emerging media.

Our Story

Built by Creators.
Driven by Production.

Cool Square Studio began with a simple belief: that technology should amplify craftsmanship, not replace it. From early experiments in 3D animation to full-scale CGI and AI-driven production, our team has grown around one discipline — telling stories that move people.

Today, that same curiosity drives everything we build, from digital humans to immersive worlds, always led by artists and refined by process.
